About

The Storyteller

Ray Revello is an independent filmmaker whose work moves fluidly between the director's chair, the producer's desk, and the actor's mark. Over two decades he has built a body of work defined by grit, humor, and a deep reverence for the craft of storytelling.

As a founding creative force at Dollars & Donuts Productions, Ray has helped shape a distinctive voice in independent cinema — one that thrives across noir, comedy, thriller, horror, and sci-fi. He is equally at home curating films, running camera and electrical, or delivering a standout performance on screen.

Self-taught and relentlessly hands-on, he brings a savant's instinct to every project: an eye for the frame, an ear for tone, and the versatility to fill whatever role a production needs.
